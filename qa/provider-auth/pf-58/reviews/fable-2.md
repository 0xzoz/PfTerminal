[P3] Credential-health ledger is per-host instance; recreated hosts lose/can't clear rejections
codex-rs/tui/src/provider_status_host.rs:141
`ProviderCredentialHealth` lives in a fresh `Arc::default()` inside each `ProviderStatusHost::from_config`. The chat widget observes rejections through the startup policy host (`model_catalog.provider_policy().host()`), while `/providers` and the recovery/auth adapters use `App::shared_provider_status_host`. That shared handle starts as `policy.host()` (same Arc) but is reset to `None` after `ProviderSetupEffect::Finish` and the deferred Corbanu-plan configured/cancelled paths (`event_dispatch.rs` ~647, ~3509, ~3539). After any of those, `open_provider_manager`/`OpenSharedProviderSetup` call `from_config` and get an empty ledger, so: (1) a later 401/`openAiAccountReauthenticationRequired` marks the policy host but `/providers` shows the provider as `Enabled · configured` even though the chat warning says to press `r`; (2) a successful re-login calls `credential_changed` on the recreated host, so the policy host keeps the provider `RecoveryRequired` and the model picker/current-selection policy stays blocked until restart, contradicting "successful replacement must permit another request without restart". Reachable only after an in-session deferred plan flow, so low severity. Smallest fix: keep the ledger process-shared (e.g., seed `provider_manager_status_host`/`OpenSharedProviderSetup` from `model_catalog.provider_policy().map(|p| p.host())` instead of `from_config`, or store the health Arc once and reuse it in `from_config`).
