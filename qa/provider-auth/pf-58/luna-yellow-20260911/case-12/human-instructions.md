# 12. Open three concurrent launcher windows

Use the app shortcut twice more. On Desktop 3, send a short request in each of the three windows and switch models in one of them.

Pass: the windows occupy distinct card slots, remain independently usable, and no launch replaces, overlaps, or moves another session.

Fail: any window lands on the wrong desktop, overlaps, reuses an existing session, or becomes unresponsive under concurrent use.
