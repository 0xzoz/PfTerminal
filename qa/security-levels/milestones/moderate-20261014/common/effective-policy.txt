on a guest found at baseline. Root-captured state: policy-root-start.txt (start) and policy-root-end.txt (end, before the
closing restore). Provisioning outputs: setup-agent.out, provision-1.out, erase-etc-codex.out, restage-replays.txt.
Harness: ../harness/ (the Aggressive scripts, paths adapted; four changes listed below).
