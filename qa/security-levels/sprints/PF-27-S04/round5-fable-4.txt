[P3] EINTR from poll/read tears down a healthy registered session as if the peer disconnected
codex-rs/secret-broker/src/linux_transport.rs:149
`watch_disconnect` treats any `poll` error (`Err(_)`) as peer closure and cancels the registered runtime session plus shuts the socket down; `read_before` likewise maps a raw `stream.read` `ErrorKind::Interrupted` to `SessionUnavailable`. On Linux `poll` is never restarted after a signal regardless of `SA_RESTART`, and std's raw `read` does not retry EINTR (only the first-byte `read_exact` in `read_prefix` does). Any signal handler installed by the future service process (SIGTERM/SIGCHLD/etc.) can therefore cancel an in-flight credentialed dispatch or drop a healthy idle session, undermining the stated "healthy idle connections stay alive" property. It is fail-closed (no security exposure) and no production service is claimed in this stage, so this is not a landing blocker; the minimal fix is to `continue` on `Errno::EINTR` in the poll loop and on `ErrorKind::Interrupted` in `read_before` while keeping the absolute deadline.
