The following intent is genuinely missing and limits exact pass criteria:

- **Freshness policy:** What makes input stale, which assessment time is authoritative, and how mixed-age evidence affects a decision’s displayed freshness.
- **Incomplete or conflicting records:** Whether an incomplete human decision remains in the queue with unknown fields, and how conflicting open/resolved records are reconciled. Neither outcome should invent certainty.
- **Resolution history:** Which history elements must be retained and for how long stable links must remain inspectable.
- **Evidence boundary:** What constitutes permissible summarized evidence versus prohibited raw private logs, including whether links to private evidence are allowed.
- **Inspection targets:** Supported desktop browsers and phone viewport sizes. These are needed to turn the browser cases into a reproducible handoff check.

These gaps do not prevent the initial cases from being prepared; they prevent imposing more specific behavior than the packet authorizes.
