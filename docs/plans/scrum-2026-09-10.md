## Operating model

The user's approved direction is **up to three independent product initiatives,
each with sequential sprints**. Builders, independent reviewers and testers may
support a sprint; they are not extra product initiatives or overlapping writers.
The smaller two-builder PF-75 pilot is an initial calibration experiment, not a
permanent ceiling on the eventual three-initiative model.

Main still enforces two active plans, allows opt-in within-plan concurrency,
and caps reserved in-progress/blocked sprints at three globally. Its two active
security sprints must receive explicit handoffs before a sequential-only policy
can truthfully describe their allocation. This docs-only merge preserves those
rules and allocations. The user's direction is approved; the policy/checker
transition and concrete lane assignments are **not landed by this merge**.

Requested supporting capabilities, tracked here for the scrum:

- **Feature flags:** incomplete product features default off, hidden from normal
  discovery and gated at actual entry points. Merging code is distinct from
  enabling it; flag-off behavior and rollback need evidence. No runtime flag is
  added or enabled by this planning publication.
- **Private progress map:** approximately half-hour updates of workstreams,
  blockers, machine assignments, agent progress, rendered sprint Markdown and
  human test-plan links. One publisher owns the view; workers submit separate
  reports instead of editing a shared dashboard document.
- **Task Node progress:** project accepted progress/evidence into the existing
  system, not a second dispatcher. Confirm the destination, write contract and
  scoped authorization; use deduplication, retry/reconciliation and visible
  failure. Do not equate a goal update with task completion or a reward claim.
  Reuse `/tasknode`, its JSON CLI and shared profile-scoped session/capture
  infrastructure. PF-75 owns explicit task/run/machine mapping, independent
  acceptance and capture-coverage/retry qualification; PF-72 adds outcome/ROI
  attribution. Basic progress does not wait for accounting. Details are in the
  [native boundary](call-followup-2026-09-10.md#native-task-node-boundary).

The private dashboard implementation/deployment and its operations plan remain
separate from this merge. This packet does not switch its source checkout,
publish it publicly, start workers or enable live Task Node writes. Its existing
private deployment is not proof those capabilities are shipped in Terminal.

## Before the first new sprint

1. Resolve the existing ledger errors and integrate the approved concurrency
   policy with explicit handoffs; confirm a free initiative and sprint slot.
2. Confirm product authority, accountable owner and independent human acceptance
   owner; make any required product-spec amendment.
3. Pin the receiving commit, distinct machine/worktree/branch, literal write
   scope, dependencies and combined-tree integration gate.
4. Freeze the user journey, feature-flag/rollback boundary, evidence requirements,
   spend/runtime caps and external-write permissions.
5. Keep at most two packets waiting for human acceptance and at most three new
   consequential questions/day; pause new starts when the review queue is full.
   Urgent security issues still escalate immediately.

The coordinator may summarize and recommend. It cannot accept its own work,
silently repair the tester's environment, substitute a requested model, or call
an unverified outcome complete. No test system is represented as bulletproof.
