How to interpret the percentages
  • “Completed” means a sprint is accepted and archived—not merely planned, drafted or partially coded.
  • Feature percentages count only that feature’s sprint records; a dependency completed in another feature does not inflate the percentage.
  • Phase and lane breakdowns retain their round-five scope; the headline count includes the later inherited profile and Task Node records.
  • The 35% integration reserve is provisional until midpoint estimates replace it with the plan’s worker-day formula.
  • Protected-mode activation remains unavailable; this dashboard reports engineering progress, not release readiness.
